# YiboLegendary 2.1.0

## 中文

### 新增传说目标追踪

- 萨弗拉斯、埃辛诺斯战刃、瓦兰奈尔、影之哀伤、巨龙之怒与龙父之牙现可追踪。
- 根据职业显示适用目标，并检测最终物品和已观察到的路线中间物品。
- 对需要收集多件最终物品的目标显示收集数量；埃辛诺斯战刃需同时获得主手与副手才算完成。
- 已获得状态在账号矩阵中显示为“已拥有”或“已完成”；悬停说明仍可查看证据细节。

### 既有功能

- 保留传说披风、雷霆之怒和索利达尔路线，以及共享账号快照和 YiboCore 入口。

**依赖：** 必须同时安装并启用 [YiboCore](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/yibocore)；本下载不包含 YiboCore。

## English

### New legendary tracking

- Added tracking for Sulfuras, Warglaives of Azzinoth, Val'anyr, Shadowmourne, Dragonwrath, and Fangs of the Father.
- Targets now show class eligibility and detect final items and observed intermediate route items.
- Multi-item targets show collection counts. Warglaives are complete only after both the main-hand and off-hand blades are observed.
- The account matrix now shows “Owned” or “Completed”; evidence details remain available in tooltips.

### Existing routes

- Legendary Cloak, Thunderfury, and Thori'dal routes remain available with shared account snapshots and YiboCore entries.

**Dependency:** [YiboCore](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/yibocore) must be installed and enabled. It is not included in this download.
