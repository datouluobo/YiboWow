# YiboTodo

**Dependencies:** Required — [YiboCore](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/yibocore) (API v5+).

## The YiboCore framework

YiboCore is the shared account window, character directory, and settings framework for Yibo addons. This addon requires [YiboCore](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/yibocore) to provide its account page and shared entry.

Check which characters have profession cooldowns or daily activities ready, from one account matrix.

## What makes it useful

Cooldowns, daily work, farm observations, and special activities share one character-by-character view, with supported actions available directly from the addon. Click an eligible profession cooldown to make one craft, use a supported item, or open the relevant profession from the matching entry. This addresses a common pain point: players can check readiness and act on supported tasks from the same place instead of tracking them separately and searching for each action.

## Features

- Profession cooldown groups such as Living Steel, Jard's Energy Source, Imperial Silk, and Celestial Cloth.
- Farm-operation observations and Nomi daily progress.
- Supported cooking, fishing, and jewelcrafting dailies, including selected older-expansion activities.
- Nat Pagle fish turn-ins, Blingtron 4000, and supported Darkmoon Faire activities.
- Click supported entries in the addon to use linked items or perform a ready profession cooldown craft; other profession states can open the matching profession.
- Configurable monitoring projects, compact previews, and account-wide status views.

## Getting started

Use `/ytd` and choose your monitoring projects. Click a supported item or ready profession cooldown to perform its available action from the addon; for other profession states, open the matching profession from the entry. Open other activities in game to collect observations, and read an icon's tooltip for its supported actions.

## Requirements and data

Mists of Pandaria Classic; packaged for client 5.5.4. Requires [YiboCore](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/yibocore), API v5 or later. Keep Core updated together with this addon.

An unobserved or outdated state remains unconfirmed. Cooldown readiness does not prove that materials are available. Farm progress is based on supported observations rather than a complete live crop scan.

# YiboTodo · 账号待办

**依赖：** 强依赖 — [YiboCore](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/yibocore)（API v5+）。



## YiboCore 框架

YiboCore 为 Yibo 插件提供统一账号窗口、角色目录与设置工作台。本插件需要 [YiboCore](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/yibocore) 承载账号页和统一入口。

用一张账号矩阵查看哪个角色的专业冷却已就绪、哪些日常和活动还需处理。

## 特点与解决的问题

分散在专业、农场和活动中的待办集中查看，并可从插件内直接执行已支持的操作：使用关联物品，或在冷却可用时点击专业项目制作一次；其它专业状态可从待办打开对应专业。
解决的痛点：不用只在待办列表核对后再到处寻找入口；查看进度的同时即可处理适用项目，也能减少跨专业、农场和活动的日常遗漏。

## 主要功能

- 追踪活化钢、贾德的特制能量源、帝王丝绸、神纹布等专业冷却组。
- 记录农场操作观测与诺米日常进度。
- 支持已收录烹饪、钓鱼与珠宝日常，包含部分旧资料片项目。
- 追踪纳特三鱼交付、布林顿 4000 及已收录暗月活动。
- 可在插件内点击使用关联物品，或对冷却可用的专业项目直接制作一次；其它状态可打开对应专业继续处理。
- 可配置监控项目与紧凑预览，在一个账号矩阵中集中查看各角色状态。

## 开始使用

使用 `/ytd` 并选择监控项目。点击支持的物品或已就绪专业冷却项目即可从插件内执行对应操作；其它专业状态可从项目入口打开对应专业。进入游戏中的其它相关活动以采集观测，悬停图标可查看支持的操作。

## 运行需求与数据说明

熊猫人之谜经典版；当前发布包对应 5.5.4 客户端。需要 [YiboCore](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/yibocore) API v5 或更新版本，建议与业务插件一起更新。

未观测或过期状态保留待确认。冷却就绪不证明材料齐全；农场进度基于已支持的操作观测，并非完整实时作物扫描。
